SoundState · Android

Privacy policy

Sound settings

SoundState reads media, ring and alarm volume levels and the current ringer mode on your device to display them and apply your changes. It does not access your microphone, record audio or read notification content.

Permissions and background controls

Some devices require Do Not Disturb access to switch into or out of Silent. When you enable background tile control, SoundState runs a foreground service and uses notification permission to show its status. You can turn this feature off in SoundState and revoke access in Android Settings.

Storage, sharing and deletion

Layout preferences and Quick Settings tile state are stored in the app’s private storage. Android may back up or transfer layout preferences according to your system settings. The app’s backup rules exclude its Quick Settings preferences. Clear SoundState’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall it to remove local app data. System sound levels are managed by Android.

Collection and third parties

The app requires no account. The developer does not receive your sound settings or app preferences, sell them, or share them with advertisers or analytics providers. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Downloads and support links also open GitHub. GitHub Privacy Statement.

Privacy questions

Contact the SoundState maintainer (kaze-hk) via the project support page. Reports are public, so please omit personal details.
